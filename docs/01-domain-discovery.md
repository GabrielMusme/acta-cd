# 1. Purpose

The purpose of this document is to discover, validate and refine the business domain before defining any software architecture.

This document intentionally precedes the Domain Model.

No implementation decisions shall be made here.

The objective is to understand the real-world business process carried out by the Council Secretary.

---

# 2. Guiding Principle

The software shall adapt to the business.

The business SHALL NOT adapt to the software.

Whenever the software model differs from the actual meeting workflow, the software model must be reconsidered.

---

# 3. Domain Layers

The project contains three independent domains.

Business Domain

↓

# 10. Open Questions

The following concepts require further analysis.

Can one Discussion belong to multiple Agenda Items?

Can one Resolution resolve multiple Motions?

Should Decisions be independent entities?

Are Attachments part of the business domain?

How should postponed Agenda Items be represented?

Can Discussions exist outside the Agenda?

Should the Secretary manually create Agenda Items before processing?
